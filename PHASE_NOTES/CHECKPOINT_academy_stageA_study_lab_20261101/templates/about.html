About Me

I am a highly motivated Biomedical Engineer with a passion for advancing medical technology through innovative applications of robotics and AI. My expertise lies in designing and developing automated systems for healthcare, with a strong foundation in control systems and data analysis. I am committed to leveraging my skills to create solutions that improve patient outcomes and streamline medical procedures.

My core interest lies in building powerful machines by creating the software, control platforms, and operating systems that power them. I have a deep interest in biomechanics and neuroscience, using these fields to shape a machine's physical form to solve specific problems. My skills in hardware design and research are a crucial part of this process, helping me visualize and refine these complex concepts into intuitive and effective solutions that truly bridge the gap between technology and human need.

I am a good communicator and team player, able to work effectively in multidisciplinary environments. I am committed to continuous learning and staying updated with the latest advancements in biomedical engineering and technology. Feel free to explore my website to learn more about my research, skills, and publications. I am open to connecting with like-minded professionals and exploring potential collaborations.

Outside of my professional interests, I am a pianist, photographer, and exploring new technologies. These hobbies not only provide a creative outlet but also inspire my work in biomedical engineering by fostering a mindset of curiosity and innovation.

Thank you for visiting my portfolio. I look forward to connecting with you!

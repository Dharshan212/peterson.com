COSINE — Choice-Oriented Streaming Insight and Navigation Engine

What COSINE does • COSINE notices when a supported streaming platform stays in the foreground for a long time. • COSINE gives a gentle check-in and asks what you would like to do next. • You can choose 10 More Min or Finish My Show. • COSINE records what happened and how long returning to another activity took. • COSINE can record movement between supported streaming platforms as the Hopper Effect. What COSINE observes • COSINE studies observable computer activity: the foreground window and its title. • COSINE does not read thoughts and does not measure the brain. • Foreground OTT time means the recognised OTT window or browser tab was active — COSINE cannot guarantee that a video was actually playing. COSINE, SINE and MIRL • COSINE: supported OTT foreground viewing and its check-ins. • SINE: distraction and focus-recovery intervention outside streaming. • MIRL: authoritative SINE episode evidence and recovery sequence.
